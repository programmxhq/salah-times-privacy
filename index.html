Privacy Policy for Salah Times

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Salah Times ("the app") is published by ProgrammX ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the app.

In short: you don't need an account, and your location stays on your device for calculating prayer times and the Qibla. Notifications are scheduled locally. The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect your device's advertising ID and related data under its own policies.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We don't run our own servers for the app, so we don't receive, store or sell your location, settings or usage.
  • We don't use any analytics service of our own.

2. Location

The app uses your device's location, with your permission, for two purposes:

  • calculating accurate prayer times, Sehri and Iftar times
  • showing the Qibla direction and your distance to the Kaaba

These calculations happen entirely on your device. Your coordinates are never sent to us. The app saves your last known location on your device so that it works offline. That saved copy is removed if you uninstall the app.

To show the name of your city, the app uses your phone's built-in geocoding service, which the operating system provides (Apple on iOS, Google on Android). That service receives your coordinates to look up a place name, and the operating system provider's privacy policy governs it. We don't receive that data.

You can refuse location permission and enter a city or coordinates manually instead. You can also revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

3. Notifications

Prayer, Sehri and Iftar reminders are local notifications. The app schedules them on your device from the prayer times it has calculated. No push-notification server is involved, and we don't collect a device token. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.

4. Settings and on-device storage

Your preferences are stored only on your device. These include:

  • calculation method, madhab and time adjustments, and the iqamah times you enter
  • theme
  • Tasbih counts and your own Tasbih target
  • notification and reminder choices
  • Quran bookmarks, last-read position and your khatm plan
  • saved duas and your progress learning the 99 Names
  • prayer and fasting tracker entries, and your qada (make-up) prayer counts
  • Zakat calculator inputs
  • the prayer-time data shown by the home-screen widget
  • nearby-mosque search results for the last 20 areas you searched (about 1 km each), refreshed after 7 days
  • whether you bought "Remove ads", so ads stay off when you are offline

We never upload them. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include them in your own Google account backup, which we can't access; you can turn this off in your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes them from the device.

4a. Online features that send limited data

Some optional features contact third-party services directly from your device. We don't operate these services and don't receive this data.

  • Nearby mosques: to search for mosques, the app sends an approximate location to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass API (overpass-api.de, or when it is unavailable one of its public mirrors, maps.mail.ru or overpass.private.coffee, and as a last resort OpenStreetMap's own search, nominatim.openstreetmap.org): the centre of the map square of about 1 km that you are in (your coordinates rounded to two decimal places), never your exact position. Everyone in the same square sends the same location. Like any internet request, it also reveals your IP address. Distances to the mosques are worked out on your device, and the results are cached there. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Zakat calculator prices: the app downloads current gold and silver prices from gold-api.com and currency exchange rates from open.er-api.com. These requests contain no personal data beyond what any internet request reveals, such as your IP address. You can enter prices manually instead.
  • Remove-ads purchase: if you buy "Remove ads", the purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We use RevenueCat to validate the purchase and restore it on your other devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app and basic device information. RevenueCat doesn't receive your name or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, which belongs to Google LLC. To serve, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted)
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate a coarse location (country or city)
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version
  • ad interactions (impressions and taps), plus diagnostic and performance data

Google processes this data according to its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
  • Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
  • Ad personalization settings: <https://adssettings.google.com>

We don't pass your GPS location to the ad network.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws). The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent, or to offer you an opt-out, before personalized ads are shown where the law requires it. You can change your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy options. That entry appears whenever it applies to your region.

iOS App Tracking Transparency. On iOS, the app asks permission before it accesses the IDFA. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are less personalized.

Opting out on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

Rewarded ads are optional. Watching one unlocks a color theme for 7 days. The only thing the app records is the unlock's expiry date, which it stores on your device.

Ad content is limited to a general-audience (PG) rating.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help.

7. Data sharing and selling

We don't sell personal information. The third parties that receive data from the app are:

  • Google, for advertising (section 5)
  • the operating system's geocoder (section 2)
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass, gold-api.com and open.er-api.com, for optional online features (section 4a)
  • the app stores and RevenueCat, only if you make a purchase (section 4a)

8. Security

Data the AdMob SDK sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • access or delete your personal data
  • object to its processing
  • withdraw consent

We don't hold any personal data about you on our servers, so most of these rights are exercised directly:

  • on your device: uninstall the app, reset your advertising ID, or use the in-app Privacy options
  • with Google, for advertising data

You can still contact us with any request and we'll help.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version, and significant changes will be noted in the app's store release notes.
